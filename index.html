Add the calendar to your phone or laptop

Google Calendar: use “Add to Google Calendar” and confirm the subscription. Changes remain managed by the school.

Apple Calendar or Outlook: subscribe using the calendar feed address for future updates. You can also download the .ics file and import it as a one-time copy. Import steps depend on your calendar application; Google Calendar file imports are done on a computer.
